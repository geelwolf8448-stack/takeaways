1. Who operates the platform

Takeaways Tonight? is an independent restaurant discovery and order-request platform for the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast. The platform is operated by an individual sole proprietor trading as Takeaways Tonight?. The legal proprietor is Antione Els, an individual sole proprietor trading as Takeaways Tonight?. The verified business/service address and public contact details have not yet been supplied. Do not submit sensitive personal information until the operator identity and privacy contact are confirmed.

2. Personal information collected

  • Customer account: email address used for sign-in, name and mobile number.
  • Customer order: restaurant selected, menu items, quantities and prices, order total, collection or delivery choice, delivery address when required, customer notes, order reference, status and whether the restaurant has confirmed direct payment.
  • Restaurant partner application: restaurant name, area, category, WhatsApp number, menu information, permission declaration and application status.
  • Restaurant owner account: sign-in email, name, mobile number, selected restaurant, requested role and access-request status.
  • Administrator activity: sign-in and application/order review actions needed to protect and operate the platform.

Do not enter card numbers, CVVs, banking passwords, one-time PINs, identity documents or special personal information into the website or order notes.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, save customer order requests, show customers their own order history, send order details to the selected restaurant, let authorised restaurant team members manage their own orders and menus, review restaurant applications and access requests, prevent misuse, and maintain platform records.

4. Who receives information

Customer order details are visible to the customer who placed the order, authorised platform administrators and authorised team members of the selected restaurant. The customer is also offered a WhatsApp message to notify the restaurant. If the customer continues into WhatsApp, WhatsApp/Meta processes that message under its own terms and privacy information.

Supabase provides the authentication and database services. GitHub Pages hosts the public website. Authentication emails are sent through the configured email-delivery service. For restaurant-owner Android push notifications, the app may send a device registration token and the signed-in owner account identifier to Supabase; Firebase Cloud Messaging/Google processes the token to deliver order alerts. Push notifications are optional and require the owner's permission. These service providers process information as needed to provide their services. Restaurant partner information is published only after administrator approval and the required permission checks.

5. Browser storage

The website uses temporary first-party browser local storage to preserve a pending basket and checkout details for up to 30 minutes while a customer completes email sign-in. This data is cleared after a successful order submission or when it expires. The website also uses session storage for temporary order retry protection, local browser storage for sample listing data, and a service worker cache to support app loading and offline assets. These browser storage features do not replace the secure account/order records stored in Supabase.

6. Hosting and international transfers

The connected Supabase database project is currently hosted in the eu-west-1 region (Ireland). Firebase Cloud Messaging/Google may also process Android device registration and notification-delivery information outside South Africa. This means some information may be stored or processed outside South Africa. The platform operator must assess and document the lawful basis, safeguards and any cross-border transfer requirements under POPIA before commercial launch.

7. Payments

Takeaways Tonight? does not currently collect customer food-order payments or store card or banking details. Customers pay the restaurant directly after the restaurant confirms the order and payment arrangements. PayFast checkout and split settlements are not active.

8. Security and access

Customer profiles and orders are protected by authenticated access controls. Customers can view their own orders; restaurant team members can view orders for restaurants to which they have been approved; platform administrators can access records required to operate the service. Commission records are restricted to platform administrators. Restaurant-owner push tokens are protected by Row Level Security and can only be registered and read by the signed-in owner for that account; server-side functions use the token to send generic order alerts. Push messages do not include customer names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, notes or order contents. The platform uses server-side order validation and does not rely on prices or order status supplied by the browser alone.

9. Retention, correction and deletion

A formal retention and deletion schedule has not yet been approved. The working POPIA register is available in the repository for completion and legal review. Push device tokens should be removed when an owner signs out, withdraws permission, loses restaurant access or the token becomes invalid. Before commercial launch, the operator must approve and publish how long account, order, application, payment and audit records are retained and how they are securely deleted or anonymised. Users should be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of information, subject to lawful record-keeping obligations.

10. Your rights and contact

Under applicable South African data-protection law, individuals may have rights to request access to or correction of personal information and to raise objections or complaints. The proposed Information Officer is Antione Els, the sole proprietor; registration with the Information Regulator is still pending. A verified privacy email/telephone contact has not yet been supplied. These details must be added before the platform is promoted as a fully operational commercial service.
