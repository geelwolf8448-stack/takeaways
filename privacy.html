Privacy notice

Draft notice for the early-stage directory · 4 November 2026

Important: This is an interim plain-language notice, not legal advice. The operator's full legal identity, privacy contact and retention schedule still need to be published before wider commercial launch.

What this preview collects

When a restaurant representative submits the partner form while the shared Supabase service is available, the platform receives the restaurant name, area, category, WhatsApp number, menu or experience items and submission status. These details are stored in the platform's Supabase database so an authorised administrator can review the application, contact the business, verify permission and decide whether to publish a listing. Applications are intended to remain private until approved. If online submission fails, the site may offer a local draft download; that does not mean the application was received.

Future shared applications

Applications submitted through the shared service are used to review the request, contact the business, verify permission and menu information, and prepare an approved directory listing. Applications should remain private and should not be displayed publicly.

Customer orders and payments

For an approved participating restaurant, customers may enter their name, mobile number, collection or delivery choice, delivery address when applicable, order items and notes. The website uses these details to prepare a WhatsApp message addressed directly to the restaurant. Takeaways Tonight? does not currently store the basket or collect the customer's payment. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply once the user continues into WhatsApp. Do not send banking passwords or one-time PINs through the platform. Live in-app payments are not available.

Data, access and security

Only information needed to operate the directory should be collected. Supabase provides the shared application and directory database. Private administrator actions require an authenticated allowlisted account. The website may use browser local storage for locally saved preview listings and a service worker cache. The site must never expose a Supabase service-role key or payment secret in public code. Do not submit passwords, bank details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through this preview.

Your choices

Before the shared service launches, the operator must publish a working contact channel for privacy questions, correction requests and deletion requests, and explain any retention periods and service providers.

Contact

A verified platform privacy contact and documented retention schedule have not yet been published. Before wider launch, the operator must provide a working channel for access, correction, deletion and privacy enquiries, explain retention periods and document the applicable lawful basis and rights under South African data-protection law. Do not submit sensitive personal information.
