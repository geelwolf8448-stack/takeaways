Privacy notice

Draft notice for the early-stage directory · 3 November 2026

Important: This is an initial plain-language notice, not legal advice. It must be reviewed and updated with the operator's real contact details and actual data practices before collecting information at scale.

What this preview collects

The current preview lets a restaurant owner enter a restaurant name, area, category, WhatsApp number and menu. At present, the form prepares a downloadable application file on the device. It does not send that file to the platform or publish the restaurant.

Future shared applications

When the shared application service is activated, submitted business details will be used to review the request, contact the restaurant, verify permission and menu information, and prepare an approved directory listing. Applications should remain private and should not be displayed publicly.

Customer orders and payments

The current preview does not accept live orders or payments. A future in-app checkout will need to process customer contact, order, payment-reference, refund and fulfilment information through approved service providers. Card details must be handled by the payment provider and must never be stored in the website database. The platform will need to publish the provider list, purposes, retention periods, security measures and customer rights before activating checkout. Do not send banking passwords or one-time PINs through the platform.

Data, access and security

Only information needed to operate the directory should be collected. Private applications must be access-controlled; public pages should show only approved business information. Do not submit passwords, bank details, identity documents or sensitive personal information through this preview.

Your choices

Before the shared service launches, the operator must publish a working contact channel for privacy questions, correction requests and deletion requests, and explain any retention periods and service providers.

Contact

A verified platform privacy contact has not yet been published. Do not treat this draft page as a monitored support channel.
