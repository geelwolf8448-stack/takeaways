Terms of use

Draft terms for the early-stage directory · 3 November 2026

Important: These are preliminary plain-language terms, not legal advice. They must be reviewed and completed with the operator's legal identity and contact details before commercial launch.

What the platform does

Takeaways Tonight? is being developed as an independent directory to help people discover South Coast food businesses, view menus and contact restaurants. It is not itself a restaurant, delivery fleet, payment processor or agent of any restaurant.

Preview information

Sample restaurant names, menus and prices are demonstrations only. They are not offers from real businesses. Do not rely on demo listings to place orders or make payments.

Restaurant listings

A business may be listed only after its identity, authority to submit information, contact number, menu and prices have been checked. Restaurant owners remain responsible for keeping their details accurate and for honouring applicable laws and customer commitments.

Orders and payment

Where direct WhatsApp ordering is available, a message is an order request, not a confirmed booking or acceptance. The restaurant must confirm availability, final price, collection or delivery, and payment arrangements. Never pay an unverified person or account.

Promotions and reviews

Promotions, ratings, reviews and sponsored placements must be clearly identified when those features are introduced. Users must not post false, abusive, unlawful or misleading content.

Availability and liability

Menus, prices, opening hours and stock can change. The platform preview is provided as-is and may contain errors or interruptions. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be excluded under South African law.

Contact and updates

A verified operator name and support contact must be added before public commercial launch. These terms may be updated as the service develops.
