1. What the platform does

Takeaways Tonight? helps people discover South Coast restaurants, view approved menus, create order requests and contact restaurants. It is a directory and technology platform, not the restaurant, food manufacturer, delivery fleet or seller of the food.

2. Accounts

Customers may create an account using a one-time email sign-in link. Account holders are responsible for keeping access to their email account secure and for providing accurate contact details. Restaurant owners and staff must request access to an approved restaurant. Access is granted only after the platform administrator has reviewed the request and verified the applicant's authority.

3. Listings and menu information

Listings marked DEMO EXAMPLE are illustrative only and cannot accept orders. A food business that completes registration confirms it is authorised to publish the submitted details; its listing is published automatically. The business is responsible for ensuring it has permission to use its name, menu, prices, photographs and contact details. Restaurants are responsible for the accuracy and currency of their menus, prices, photographs, contact details, availability, allergen information and fulfilment arrangements. Prices and availability can change and must be confirmed with the restaurant.

4. Customer orders

Customers can submit an order request from an approved restaurant's menu. The platform saves the request to the customer's account and provides a WhatsApp message so the customer can notify the restaurant. The restaurant portal allows approved restaurant team members to review and update order status.

A submitted order is not an accepted order. The restaurant must expressly accept or reject it and confirm availability, final price, delivery charges, collection/delivery arrangements and timing. The platform does not guarantee that a restaurant will accept an order or fulfil it.

5. Payment

Takeaways Tonight? does not currently collect customer food-order payments. Customers pay the restaurant directly after the restaurant confirms the order and payment arrangements. Do not treat an order request or WhatsApp message as proof of payment.

Takeaways Tonight does not deduct a percentage or order-based platform fee from restaurant food sales. Customers pay the restaurant directly after the restaurant confirms the order and payment arrangements. Restaurants retain 100% of their food-sale revenue. Any future change to this arrangement would require advance disclosure and written agreement.

PayFast checkout, split payments, automated refunds and restaurant payouts are not active. They will not be enabled until the payment provider approves the proposed arrangement and settlement, cancellation, refund and reconciliation procedures have been tested and disclosed.

6. Restaurant responsibilities

Restaurants remain responsible for food preparation, food safety, product descriptions, allergen information, pricing, stock, acceptance or rejection of orders, delivery or collection, customer service, refunds and compliance with applicable laws. Restaurant owners must keep access limited to authorised staff and promptly remove access for people who leave their business. The Android restaurant app may offer optional push notifications for new order requests. Delivery depends on device settings, network availability and Firebase/Google services and is not guaranteed; restaurant owners remain responsible for monitoring their orders.

7. Promotions and content

Users and restaurant partners must not submit unlawful, misleading, infringing, abusive or false content. Sponsored listings and paid promotions must be clearly identified if introduced. The platform may remove inaccurate or unauthorised listings.

8. Availability and liability

The website and app may be updated, interrupted or unavailable. Menus, prices, hours and stock may be out of date. Nothing in these terms removes consumer or other rights that cannot legally be excluded under South African law.

9. Restaurant subscriptions

Restaurants receive a 14-day free pilot. If they choose to continue after the pilot, the subscription is a once-off R250 joining/setup fee followed by R500 per month. No commission or percentage is deducted from food orders; restaurants retain 100% of food-sale revenue. The subscription start date, billing method, service scope, cancellation process and refund terms must be confirmed in writing with the restaurant. Subscription payments are not currently collected through this website.
