## 5. Customer FAQ

**Is an order confirmed when I submit it?**
No. The request is saved to your account and WhatsApp opens so you can notify the restaurant. The restaurant must confirm availability, final total and collection or delivery arrangements.

**Can I pay in the app?**
Not yet. No in-app food payment is currently available. Confirm payment arrangements directly with the restaurant using a channel you trust.

**Does Takeaways Tonight? deliver food?**
The current platform does not operate a delivery fleet. Each restaurant confirms whether it offers delivery and its own delivery fees and area.

**Are demo restaurants real partners?**
No. Demo cards are examples only and cannot accept orders. Only owner-approved and published businesses are participating partners.

**What if a menu price is wrong?**
The restaurant must confirm the current menu, stock and final price before accepting an order. Report outdated information through the verified platform contact once it is published.
