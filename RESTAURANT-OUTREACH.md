# Restaurant partner outreach — Takeaways Tonight?

## Short WhatsApp introduction

Hi! I'm getting in touch about **Takeaways Tonight?**, a new South Coast food directory helping local people discover takeaway menus and restaurant specials around Margate, Shelly Beach, Uvongo and Port Shepstone.

We're inviting a small group of local restaurants to help us launch. The idea is simple: customers can find your menu and contact your restaurant directly to place an order request.

There is no listing fee being advertised at this early stage. We would first need your permission, your current menu and prices, and the correct restaurant WhatsApp number. Nothing is published until you approve the details.

You can view the preview here:
https://geelwolf8448-stack.github.io/takeaways/

Would you be interested in chatting about becoming one of our first local restaurant partners?

## If the owner asks what they need to provide

- Business name and location
- Food category
- Current menu and prices
- Restaurant WhatsApp number for customer enquiries
- Permission to display their business details, menu and prices
- Opening hours and collection/delivery arrangements (for the listing information)

## Important promises — do not overstate

- The website is an early-stage preview.
- Demo restaurant cards are not real partners.
- The first version sends customers to the restaurant; it does not process payments or arrange drivers.
- A WhatsApp message is an order request. The restaurant confirms availability, final price, collection/delivery and payment.
- Do not promise increased sales, guaranteed orders, exclusive promotion or a permanent free listing.
- Do not publish a restaurant's name, menu, logo, photos or contact details without permission.

## Suggested first target

Start with a small, manageable group: approach 3 restaurants in Margate, then 2 in Shelly Beach, 2 in Uvongo and 3 in Port Shepstone. Track who was contacted, who replied, and who gave permission. Do not add unverified listings just to make the directory look busy.
