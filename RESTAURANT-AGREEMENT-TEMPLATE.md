## 2. Service being agreed

Takeaways Tonight? provides a restaurant discovery listing, menu display, customer accounts and an order-request workflow. A signed-in customer can submit a basket; the platform saves the order request to the customer's account and offers a WhatsApp message to notify the restaurant. Approved restaurant team members can request portal access and, once verified, manage their own menu and order statuses.

The order and WhatsApp message are only requests. The restaurant must confirm whether it accepts the request, item availability, current prices, any delivery fee, fulfilment method and timing. The platform does not currently collect customer food payments, provide a delivery fleet, guarantee orders or act as the restaurant's food seller.

## 4. Restaurant responsibilities

The restaurant is responsible for:
- Keeping its listing, menu, prices, opening hours, availability and contact details accurate.
- Confirming all orders directly with customers.
- Food preparation, hygiene, allergens, packaging, collection, delivery, delivery fees and applicable licences.
- Confirming the final amount and payment method directly with the customer.
- Handling complaints, cancellations, refunds and fulfilment for its own sales.
- Promptly notifying the platform of changes, closures, withdrawn permission or any incorrect listing information.

## 5. Platform responsibilities

The platform operator will:
- Publish the approved listing after reviewing the submitted permission and details.
- Use reasonable care when transferring approved information to the listing.
- Provide a contact channel for corrections and removal requests once operator contact details are completed.
- Not represent the restaurant as a partner before approval.
- Not promise a minimum number of views, leads, orders, revenue or sales.
