# Takeaways Tonight — current project status

**Public site:** https://geelwolf8448-stack.github.io/takeaways/

## Customer experience

- Customers use the phone-friendly public app to discover published restaurants near their current location, browse menus, add quantities and send an order request.
- Customer food payments go directly to the chosen restaurant using that restaurant's listed payment methods. Takeaways Tonight does not take a commission from food sales.
- The customer directory shows only real, published restaurant records. It does not show the private restaurant-owner training example.

## Restaurant owner experience

- Restaurant owners use the laptop/web dashboard to add a logo, menu categories, food photos, descriptions and prices, choose direct payment methods, manage orders and save their business location.
- Exactly one private restaurant-owner demo is available for dashboard practice. It is not a customer listing, search result, menu or order destination.
- Every restaurant receives a 14-day free pilot. The pilot dates can be viewed in the owner dashboard.

## Payment reset — 15 November 2026

- The former platform payment provider has been removed from the customer app, restaurant dashboard, admin dashboard and registration flow.
- The old online checkout and notification receiver are disabled. The former notification address now rejects requests and cannot accept or settle a payment.
- New business registration still starts the free pilot, but it does not create a platform invoice or schedule a platform charge.
- No platform payment provider is active. No automatic debit, subscription checkout or platform payment collection is running.
- A replacement platform payment system will only be introduced after the owner chooses the method, confirms the business/payee details and the full flow is tested. Until then, there is nothing for a restaurant to pay through the app.

## Before public introduction

1. Add and verify at least one real restaurant that has given written permission for its logo, menu, prices and contact details to be published.
2. Test the full customer journey on a phone: location permission, nearby listing, menu, basket, order request and the restaurant's direct payment instruction.
3. Test the full owner journey on a laptop: sign-in, logo, categories, menu items, photos, prices, payment methods and order status.
4. Confirm the private owner training demo is invisible from every customer page, search result and ordering flow.
5. Complete the required business contact, privacy, POPIA/PAIA and launch checks before advertising the service publicly.
6. Choose and set up a fresh platform continuation-payment method only when the commercial terms and legal payee details are ready.

## Safety rules

- Do not publish a restaurant, menu, photo, price or contact number without the owner's permission and verification.
- Do not promise delivery, confirmed orders, customer volume or payment processing that is not active.
- Do not store merchant logins, passwords, recovery codes or payment secrets in the website, repository or chat.
